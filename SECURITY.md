# Security Policy

## Supported versions

EngramMesh has not made its first release. Supported versions will be listed
here after the first release; until then, no version is designated as supported
for security fixes.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Vulnerabilities must not be filed as public issues. Please report them privately
by email to [s.bing@qq.com](mailto:s.bing@qq.com), or through [GitHub Private
Vulnerability Reporting](https://github.com/EngramMesh/EngramMesh/security/advisories/new).

Please include enough detail to reproduce and assess the issue, such as the
affected component, impact, steps or a proof of concept, and any known
mitigations. Do not include sensitive details in public Issues, Discussions,
pull requests, or commits.

## Scope

This policy covers vulnerabilities in EngramMesh's documented architecture and
future implementation, including:

- tenant isolation;
- memory poisoning;
- tool authorization;
- sandbox escape;
- provider credentials; and
- supply chain integrity.

## Response targets

The maintainers aim to acknowledge a private report within three business days
and complete initial triage within seven calendar days. These are response
targets, not guarantees: report complexity, availability, and coordination
needs can affect timing.

## Coordinated disclosure

The reporter and maintainers will coordinate investigation, remediation, and
disclosure. When appropriate, maintainers will use GitHub's Security Advisory
workflow to track the report privately, prepare an advisory, credit the
reporter with permission, and publish disclosure details after affected users
have a reasonable opportunity to act. The timing and content of any disclosure
will be agreed during coordination and may be adjusted to reduce risk.
