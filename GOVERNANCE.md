# EngramMesh Governance

EngramMesh is stewarded in the open by the `@EngramMesh/maintainers` team for the `EngramMesh/EngramMesh` repository. Decisions should prioritize the project's documented architecture, contributor safety, and long-term maintainability.

## Roles

### Contributor

A Contributor proposes issues, RFCs, documentation, tests, or code and follows the contribution and conduct policies. Contributors may participate in public discussion and review.

### Reviewer

A Reviewer provides substantive, respectful feedback on pull requests, ADRs, or RFCs. Reviewers help verify correctness, tests, documentation, compatibility, and policy compliance; they do not merge changes solely by virtue of this role.

### Maintainer

Maintainers are members of `@EngramMesh/maintainers`. They facilitate public decision-making, review and merge contributions, enforce project policies, and maintain the repository's security and release responsibilities.

## Decisions and approvals

Project decisions are made through issues, pull requests, ADRs, RFCs, and published meeting notes, with the most durable decision record linked from the change. Ordinary changes require appropriate review by a Maintainer.

Changes involving licensing, a security boundary, compatibility, or governance require approval from two Maintainers before merge or adoption. Where an RFC is required, the approval is recorded on its public discussion or implementing pull request.

Maintainers must disclose actual, potential, or perceived conflicts of interest that could materially affect a decision. A conflicted Maintainer should not be counted toward the required approval when a non-conflicted Maintainer is available.

## Stewardship and continuity

Maintainer activity is reviewed after 180 days without visible project participation. The review is an opportunity to confirm continued availability, update access, and invite the Maintainer to resume participation when ready. Maintainers may voluntarily move to emeritus status; emeritus Maintainers retain recognition but do not hold active repository authority unless reinstated.

Project decisions made in meetings must have public meeting notes, including the decision, participants, rationale, and links to any relevant issue, ADR, or RFC. Sensitive security details may be redacted while preserving a public record of the decision and rationale to the extent safely possible.
